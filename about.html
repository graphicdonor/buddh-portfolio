I believe great design is invisible. It removes friction, earns trust, and makes complex workflows feel obvious. I design for the people who use the product every day — not for award screenshots.

What I Believe

Design principles
I live by

🧠
Systems over screens
Every screen is a consequence of a system. I start with workflows, mental models, and data architecture — the UI follows naturally from that foundation.
📊
Design for outcomes
Beautiful is worthless if it doesn't ship or move a metric. I tie every design decision to a measurable business or user outcome — and hold myself accountable to it.
🤝
Collaboration is the craft
The best products come from engineering, product, and design thinking as one team — not throwing specs over a wall. I embed in the team, not alongside it.
🚀
Ship, then improve
Iteration beats perfection every time. I design for the fastest path to learning — a shipped MVP with feedback beats a polished prototype with assumptions.
🤖
AI as a design material
AI isn't a feature — it's a new design primitive. I design interaction paradigms where intelligence is the default, not an afterthought layered on top.
🌐
Inclusion by default
Accessibility and multilingual support aren't extras — they're requirements. I build products for the full spectrum of users from the very first wireframe.
Philosophy
"Systems thinking > screens.
AI should drive decisions.
Integration is the real moat."
01
Understand the whole system
Before opening Figma, I map the entire workflow — every stakeholder, every handoff, every failure point. The design is already half-done by the time I touch a tool.
02
Let AI carry the cognitive load
The best interfaces minimize decisions. With AI, we can automate judgment, surface the right action at the right moment, and let humans focus on what only they can do.
03
Integration beats features
A product that connects deeply with existing workflows is 10× harder to replace than one with more features. I design for depth of integration, not breadth of capability.
