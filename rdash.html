01 / Problem

Construction workflows are deeply fragmented

Teams ran critical workflows across Excel for BOQs, WhatsApp for communication, ERPs for finance, and manual on-site reporting — resulting in zero real-time visibility, delayed decisions, heavy cost leakages, and crushing operational overhead.

📊
Excel BOQs & Manual Data
Spreadsheets with no live pricing, no vendor linking, and no version control — causing costly estimation errors and rework at every project stage.
💬
WhatsApp-Driven Communication
Critical decisions buried in chat threads with no accountability, no audit trail, and no way to track approvals or escalations across teams.
🔍
Zero Real-Time Visibility
Stakeholders had no live view of project progress, budget consumption, or procurement status — everything was reactive, never proactive.
💸
High Cost Leakages
Disconnected billing, payment disputes, and manual reconciliation loops led to significant financial leakage across every active project.
02 / Solution

Construction OS — one unified platform

R'Dash connects every layer of a construction project into a single intelligent system — from procurement to payments, site execution to AI insights — eliminating tool-switching and giving every stakeholder exactly what they need.

03 / Key Initiatives

Platform leadership across every layer

01
My Projects Dashboard
Designed a high-density project command centre tracking 768+ live projects across 8 pipeline stages — Project Created, Recce Pending, Design Pending, Designs Approved, BOQ in Process, BOQ Approved, Execution in Progress, Execution Completed. Stage-badge filtering, multi-tag search, and a sortable data table (Job ID, Client, City, Project Manager, Estimate, Due Date) gave managers full fleet visibility in one view.
768+ projects tracked8 pipeline stages7-day go-live
02
Recce & Mobile Site Survey
Replaced manual WhatsApp site-survey coordination with a structured Recce module. PMs create a template-driven recce link on web (External Environment, Internal Environment, Signage & Facade, Layouts & Sketches, Custom Sections), assign it to field engineers, and the link is shared via WhatsApp. Engineers execute the survey on mobile — uploading photos, videos, and structured data. The web reviewer approves or rejects each section. Eliminated untracked site data and cut survey-to-approval time significantly.
Mobile-first captureWhatsApp link sharingStructured approval flow
03
BOQ & Scope Management
Designed a sectioned BOQ system replacing Excel — Civil Work, Demolition & Dismantling, Interior & Allied Work — with image-linked line items, category codes, source proposals (PROP432545), item type tags (Procurement / Production), UOM, quantity, and final amount per line. Automatic footer totals show Base Amount, Avg. Discount %, Total Discount, and Net Amount in real time. Tabs for My Scope, Proposals For Client, Incoming Orders, and Rejected Scope gave every stakeholder a filtered view of exactly their responsibility.
65% less manual invoice effort40% fewer disputesAuto net amount calc
04
Vendor Orders & Payment Management
Designed a full vendor procurement layer — Proposals From Vendor, Vendor Order, Vendor Invoices, Vendor Payments, Other Expenses, Vendor Wise Scope, and Vendor Wise Progress Report. Each order tracks Order No., Order To, Issued By, Issue/Due Dates, Order Status (Pending Approval / Pending), PO attachment status, and a visual progress bar per order. Category tags (Production, Procurement, Tilling, Flooring) enable filtering across 24+ line items per vendor. Eliminated PO-to-payment ambiguity that caused reconciliation delays and cost leakages.
End-to-end PO tracking7 vendor sub-modules50% less implementation effort
05
Work Progress Tracking & Snag Management
Rebuilt progress visibility with a dual-view system — a Gantt-style Date Tracker (Projected End vs. Expected Due) and a Time Status bar showing Actual Start, Till Date, and Expected Due at a glance. Section-level progress % (e.g. 65%) rolls up to daily (12%) and overall (30%) summaries with rupee values. The Snag module adds a quality control layer: field teams log snags with photo evidence, location, category tags (Faucet Missing, Piper Fitting), and committed closure dates — tracked through Open Unresolved → Open Resolved → Closed states with a tri-color progress bar.

05 / UX Strategy

AI-first, systems-led design

Four principles governed every design decision across the platform — from onboarding flows to AI Copilot to offline execution.

AI-First Interaction
Natural language queries, predictive suggestions, and automated workflows as the default — not the advanced option. Every user gets the benefit of AI without needing to configure it.
Progressive Disclosure
Strict information hierarchy with contextual reveal — complexity only appears when a user is ready for it. Construction workflows are complex; the UI shouldn't amplify that.
Role-Based UX
Tailored views for each stakeholder — developer, contractor, site manager. Reduce noise by surfacing only what's relevant to each role's daily decisions and responsibilities.
Mobile + Offline First
Every screen designed for a construction site — works on mobile, degrades gracefully to full offline. Desktop was secondary; field reality was always the first constraint.
