01 / Problem

Reading is social but apps treat it as solitary

Goodreads is the dominant player but it's dated, slow, and focused on cataloguing over community. Book lovers have rich conversations on Twitter and Reddit but there's no dedicated space for discovery, shared highlights, and community-driven recommendations.

🔍
No Social Discovery
No way to find readers with similar taste. Discovery is either algorithmic or manual search — neither reflects the taste of people you actually trust.
📖
No Highlight Sharing
Readers already screenshot passages to share on social. There's no native space for this — no way to share meaningful highlights where book lovers congregate.
🤖
Algorithmic Recommendations
Generic "users also liked" suggestions that don't account for nuance or trust. An algorithm recommending a book carries a fraction of the weight of a friend's recommendation.
⌛
Dated UX
Goodreads hasn't evolved in years. The interface is cluttered, slow, and built around cataloguing — not community. A generation of readers has grown up expecting better.
02 / User Research

Understanding the reader mindset

I ran 12 user interviews with avid readers — 3 to 10 books per year — across fiction and non-fiction. Three themes emerged consistently and shaped every core design decision.

Reading as Identity
People want their taste to be visible. What you read is an expression of who you are — genre, pace, depth. Your profile should show that, not hide it behind a static shelf.
Discovery Through Trust
Recommendations from people you know carry far more weight than algorithms. "A friend loved it" beats five stars from strangers — every time, for every reader interviewed.
Highlights as Currency
Readers already screenshot passages to share on social. Highlighting is how they signal depth and appreciation. Making this a first-class action turns it into social glue.
03 / Core Design Decisions

Four things that made Knovelo different

01
Feed-First Architecture
Home is a social feed of highlights and reviews from people you follow — not a catalogue. Discovery happens through people, not search. The feed is the product, not a secondary feature.
Social feed as homePeople over search
02
Highlight-Native Interaction
Highlighting a passage is a first-class action — not a buried feature. You can quote a highlight in a review, reshare it, or react to others' highlights directly. The passage becomes the unit of social currency.
First-class highlight actionQuote · Share · React
03
Taste Profile
Your profile shows your genre spread, reading speed, favourite authors, and recent highlights — making your taste visible and matchable. Identity is the discovery engine, not just a vanity page.
Genre spread visualisedTaste matchable
04
Trust Circles
Follow friends, trusted reviewers, and public figures. Their ratings carry more weight than aggregate scores in your personalised view. Social trust, not crowd consensus, drives what surfaces for you.
Weighted trust scoringPersonal over aggregate
04 / Key Screens

The flows that matter

Four core screens carry the full Knovelo experience — each designed around a distinct user job, with mobile-first interaction patterns and a content hierarchy built for scanning.

Onboarding
Taste quiz — genre picks and reading pace — followed by follow suggestions seeded from your selections. First value in under 3 minutes: a personalised feed before you've read a word.
Feed
Highlight cards, review cards, and discovery prompts interspersed in a single infinite scroll. Each card is optimised for quick reading — author, book, passage, and context at a glance.
Book Detail
Community rating from your trust circle plus highlights and reviews from people you follow, topped with algorithmically related picks. The social layer wraps every book, not just popular ones.
Profile
Taste graph showing genre spread, reading history timeline, and a highlight wall. Your profile becomes a readable expression of your literary identity — discoverable by people who share your taste.
05 / Outcome

A reading experience that feels social

Knovelo went from a blank canvas to a production-ready iOS and Android design — with a complete interaction system, documented component library, and a social reading experience that treats community as the core product.
