01 / Problem

Brands couldn't hear what the world was saying about them

Brand monitoring tools were either too expensive for growing companies or too technical for non-analysts to use. WebSignals was built to democratise access to real-time brand intelligence — without requiring a data science team to run it.

💸
High Cost of Existing Tools
Brandwatch and Mention priced out of reach for growing companies. The gap between free Google Alerts and enterprise tools left most brands unprotected.
📊
No Real-Time Sentiment for SMBs
Sentiment analysis was locked behind enterprise contracts and required analysts to interpret results. SMBs had no accessible way to understand brand health at a glance.
🔧
Difficult Setup for Non-Technical Users
Setting up keyword tracking across sources required technical configuration that marketing managers couldn't do alone. Adoption died at setup.
🚨
No Alert System for Crisis Moments
By the time brands discovered a PR crisis, it had already spread. No real-time alerting system existed for sudden spikes in negative sentiment or mention volume.

Positioning
Real-time brand monitoring for growing B2B brands who need enterprise-grade intelligence at an accessible price point — without a data science team.
Core Jobs
Track brand mentions across web and social. Detect sentiment in real time. Alert on crisis moments before they compound. Three jobs, one platform.
MVP Scope
Web monitoring + social listening + real-time alert system. Scoped ruthlessly to ship fast and validate before expanding to deeper analytics features.
03 / Design System

The foundation that makes scale possible

Built the design system before the first screen. A systematic approach to colour, typography, and components meant that as the product grew, every new feature inherited the same quality floor — without rework.

Color System
Brand palette plus semantic tokens for status, sentiment, and severity. Positive/neutral/negative had distinct colour signatures — consistent across every chart, feed item, and alert.
Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans throughout with a clear display hierarchy — headline, body, data labels, and micro-copy each with distinct size, weight, and line-height rules.
Alert Components
Real-time feed items with severity levels, notification states, and actionable CTAs. Designed for high-density information scanning without visual overwhelm.
Dashboard Widgets
Mention volume sparklines, sentiment trend charts, source breakdown donuts, and top-keyword lists — each a reusable, composable widget for any dashboard layout.
