My Process

How I approach every project

01
Discover & Define
Stakeholder interviews, user research, and competitive analysis to deeply understand the problem space before opening Figma.
02
Map & Structure
Journey mapping, IA, and workflow definition to create the skeleton of the experience — structure before aesthetics, always.
03
Design & Iterate
High-fidelity mockups with interactive prototypes — validated with real users through structured, moderated testing sessions.
04
Systematize
Building reusable component libraries and design tokens that scale across the entire product — not just the current feature.
05
Ship & Measure
Close collaboration with engineering during handoff, then measuring real usage data and analytics to inform the next iteration.
06
Reflect & Refine
Post-launch retrospectives and continuous improvement cycles — treating launch as the beginning of the design process, not the end.
